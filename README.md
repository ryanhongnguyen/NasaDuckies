# 🌱 No Drip: Empowering Smarter Farming Decisions 💧
No Drip empowers farmers by providing real-time insights on water and nutrient needs, tailored specifically to their farms. Our app guides actionable agricultural decisions while fostering a knowledge-sharing community. Together, we can "grow more, with less," ensuring that not a single drop of water goes to waste. 🌍

# Problem Overview
Innovative solutions that empower farmers to leverage data-driven insights from trusted resources—including NASA Earth data—are needed to tackle challenges related specifically to water. Farming and agronomy are already full-time jobs; a means for the agricultural community to take advantage of the free Earth observation data NASA offers without needing to become data scientists could reap tremendous benefits.

# Current irrigation practices
Every year, the US uses an estimated 322 billion gallons of water daily for agricultural uses (1)

# 🚀 App Key Features 
 1. 💦 Water Use Recommendations -- Receive tailored, real-time water insights for each crop, and automate watering with real-time conditions
 2. 🌍 Leveraging Community Insights -- Learn about the impact of climate change on agriculture and access expert advice through shared insights
 3. 🌾 Customizable Farm Profiles -- Share crops, connect with potential neighbors, and get personalized advice based on your unique profile
 4. 🗺️ Real-Time Map That Introduce Gamification -- View your farm with real-time weather data and collaborate with nearby farmers to tackle shared challenges
